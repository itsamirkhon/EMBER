## 2. Audit of All 9 Mandatory Fixes

### Fix 1 — Replace Binary `BURN` Collapse with Stage-Specific Protocol Segments & Events
- **Legacy Defect (`web/src/lib/evidence.ts:84-93`)**: `BURN = new Set(['burned', 'flame_spread', 'completed', 'disruption'])` and `NOBURN = new Set(['extinguished', 'self_extinguished', 'no_ignition'])` flatten rich experimental timelines into one binary scalar (`1` or `0`). Examples of scientific distortion:
  - **BASS / BASS-II**: A test that ignites, spreads steadily at 5 cm/s, and is intentionally terminated by operator `flow_shutoff` (`BASS-II-B1`) or extinguished only after flow is stepped down (`BASS-II-M1`, `BASS-II-P18`) or `N2` jet injection is collapsed into `'flame_spread'`/`'burned'` with zero record of which stage extinguished or continued.
  - **CFI**: Droplets that undergo radiative hot-flame extinction (`DextHotMm`) and then continue burning via low-temperature **cool flames** (`kCoolMm2S`, `DextCoolMm`) are reduced to `'extinguished'`, hiding active cool-flame combustion.
  - **SAME / SAME-R / SPICE**: Non-flaming smoke generation or steady jet smoke-point tests were forced into `'unknown'` or `'not_applicable'`.
- **EMBER v2 Resolution**: Every `Test` carries ordered `segments: ProtocolStep[]` (`preparation`, `ignition`, `steady_burn`, `flow_modification`, `gas_intervention`, `aging`, `termination`) and stage-bound `events: TestEvent[]` mapped to typed `TargetEndpoint`s (`flame_establishment`, `sustained_spread`, `extinction_under_flow_change`, `blowoff_limit`, `quench_limit`, `spread_rate`, `burning_rate_constant`, `hot_flame_extinction`, `cool_flame_continuation`, `smoke_particle_size`, `smoke_point_length`, `suppressant_extinction`). Unknown source timestamps (`timeStartS`, `timeEndS`) remain `null` and are never fabricated.

### Fix 2 — Represent Geometry, Thickness/Width/Confinement, Gas Roles, Flow History, Gravity Mode & Endpoint
- **Legacy Defect (`web/src/lib/types.ts`, `web/src/lib/evidence.ts:122-159`, `pipeline/build_dataset.py`)**:
  - `BASS` PMMA sphere tests (`BASS-15-54`..`23-33`, `80-98`..`91-28`) had `geometry: null`; `BASS-II` `sidesBurning` (1-sided insulated vs 2-sided burning) and `sampleWidthCm` were buried in untyped `extra` strings.
  - `ACME-CFI-G` inverse flames (`CFI-G-I-*`) stored burner oxidizer-stream `O2` in the same `o2Pct` column as ambient `O2` (`CFI-G-N-*`) with no machine-checked physical role.
  - Multi-velocity flow histories (`Air display`: `"10  8"`, `"20  10  5  2  1"`, `"2  0  2  0  etc"`) and flow direction (`concurrent` vs `opposed` vs `axial_stagnation` vs `coflow` vs `quiescent`) were flattened to initial `flowCmS`.
  - `SAME`/`SAME-R` and `SPICE` nominal ISS cabin air (`21% O2`, `101.3 kPa`) looked identical to sensor-measured `o2Pct`/`pressureKpa`.
- **EMBER v2 Resolution**:
  - All continuous quantities use `ScientificValue` (`value`, `unit`, `originalValue`, `originalUnit`, `provenance`: `reported | series_level | derived | assumed | unknown`, `sourceRef`).
  - `atmosphere.o2.role` is explicit: `"oxidizer_stream"` for `ACME-CFI-G` inverse flames (`CFI-G-I-*`) and `"ambient"` elsewhere.
  - `SAME` and `SAME-R` `atmosphere.o2` and `atmosphere.pressure` carry `provenance: "assumed"` (nominal ISS cabin air); `SPICE` nominal cabin assumptions carry `provenance: "series_level"`/`"assumed"` matching `SPICE Final Report Table 1`.
  - `Test` exposes `widthCm`, `substrateConfinement`, `orientation`, `flow.direction`, `flow.history`, `flow.sequenceCmS`, and `gravity.{gravityG, regime, platform, mode}`.

### Fix 3 — Include Material & Gravity (Regime + Mode) in `findingStatus` and `proximity`
- **Legacy Defect (`web/src/lib/evidence.ts:598-675`)**: `proximity()` explicitly overrides `{ ...scenario, fuelPhase: null, fuel: null }` and computes `testDistance(..., { ...DEFAULT_ESTIMATE_OPTIONS, gravityAware: false })`, and `findingStatus()` (`L622-675`) checks `conditionBox` intervals on O2/pressure/flow without checking `fuel`, `geometry`, `thicknessMm`, `gravityRegime`, or `gravityMode`. As a result, liquid droplet or PMMA rod data could certify `"well-supported / known"` status for a completely different solid fabric or partial-gravity premise.
- **EMBER v2 Resolution**: Applicability rules (`RULE-MAT-01`, `RULE-GEOM-01`, `RULE-GRAV-01`, `RULE-GAS-01`, `RULE-PROT-01`) require material/phase/geometry compatibility, gas-role compatibility, and explicit gravity regime/mode matching before any test can be marked `admissible`.

### Fix 4 — Joint Multi-Dimensional Domain Support vs. 1D Marginal `conditionBox`
- **Legacy Defect (`web/src/lib/evidence.ts:571-596, 622-650`)**: `conditionBox()` builds independent 1D min/max intervals `[min, max]` for `o2Pct`, `pressureKpa`, and `flowCmS`. A query can fall inside each 1D range marginally (e.g., high `O2` tested only at low pressure + quiescent flow combined with high flow tested only at `21% O2`) and falsely receive `findingStatus = 'known'`.
- **EMBER v2 Resolution**: `RULE-JOINT-01` and `marginalOverlapButJointMissing` test joint multi-dimensional proximity against actual observed test vectors rather than marginal bounding-box corners.

### Fix 5 — Remove Uncalibrated kNN `pBurn` Probabilities & Wilson Intervals
- **Legacy Defect (`web/src/lib/evidence.ts:259-405`)**: `estimate()` runs an IDW kNN binary classifier over heterogeneous endpoints and plugs synthetic `pBurn` and `nEff` into a Wilson score interval, presenting poorly calibrated binary burn probabilities with confidence intervals across non-exchangeable regimes and protocols.
- **EMBER v2 Resolution**: Binary `pBurn` prediction is removed. Quantitative estimation is restricted to continuous endpoints (`spreadRateMmS`, `particleSizeNm`, `smokePointLengthMm`, `burningRateMm2S`) via `domain_gated_knn_ridge`, gated by applicability rules, grouped cross-validation (`groupId`), and strict `beatsBaseline` checks against investigation/global mean baselines (`refused_out_of_domain` / `refused_insufficient_data` / `refused_below_baseline`).

### Fix 6 — Remove Arbitrary `4% O2` Conservatism Margin & `E-FIELD` Partial-Gravity Misattribution
- **Legacy Defect (`web/src/pages/Gravity.tsx:47, 200-208, 224-235`)**:
  - `const marginO2 = 4` is applied uniformly as `scenario.o2Pct + marginO2`, generalizing a specific upward-1g vs 0g flow-tunnel observation (`c-0137`, `ntrs-20080034883`) to arbitrary spacecraft atmospheres, fuels, and flow histories.
  - `Gravity.tsx:225-235` classifies `ACME-E-FIELD` under `"E-FIELD Flames: simulated partial gravity"`, directly contradicting `psi-22` (`c-0125`: microgravity isolates electric-field body forces from buoyancy; all rows are ISS microgravity, not partial-gravity).
- **EMBER v2 Resolution**: No arbitrary `+4% O2` offset is injected into conditions or applicability checks, and `ACME-E-FIELD` carries `gravity: { gravityG: 0, regime: "microgravity", platform: "ISS", mode: "actual_flight" }` with electric-field body-force notes separated from partial-gravity analogues.

### Fix 7 — Separate Planned vs. Observed Evidence & Fix All 8 Audited Partial/Planned Claims
- **Legacy Defect (`legacy_seed/extraction_eval.json`, `legacy_seed/investigations.json`, `legacy_seed/claims.json`)**:
  - `FLEX-2` (`PSI-68`) was stored alongside flown datasets with 0 test rows and no `epistemicStatus: "planned_only"` boundary.
  - `claims.json` lacked `epistemicStatus` and contained 8 audited partial/planned defects identified in `extraction_eval.json`:
    1. **`c-0021`** (`BASS-II`, `ntrs-20150008962`): Source covers **both BASS and BASS-II** concurrent-flow SIBAL tests (not `BASS-II` in isolation). Fixed attribution/conditionsScope/limitations + expanded verbatim quote.
    2. **`c-0037`** (`Saffire-I`, `ntrs-20180005168`): Source summarizes the **Saffire series** broadly, not Saffire-I alone; quote corrected to verbatim NTRS sentence (`"The downloaded test data show resolved..."`) with multi-flight attribution note.
    3. **`c-0041`** (`Saffire-I`, `ntrs-20150000894`): Source is a **2015 pre-flight plan** (`"On two flights (Saffire-1 and Saffire-3), the experiment will consist..."`); set `epistemicStatus: "planned"`, clarify scope covers both Saffire-1 and Saffire-3 pre-flight designs.
    4. **`c-0045`** (`Saffire-II`, `psi-99`): Legacy quote covered only the 9-sample / `5 cm x 29 cm` sentence while omitting the preceding sentence listing materials (`"including SIBAL, silicone, structure PMMA, flat PMMA, and PMMA with Nomex"`). Expanded quote to include both verbatim sentences.
    5. **`c-0101`** (`CFI`, `psi-39`): `psi-39` Objective states `0.25 atm to 5 atm`, whereas `psi-39` Approach states `0.25 to 0.5 atm`! Also legacy quote omitted Objective 2 (`helium dilution`). Expanded quote to cover Objectives 1–2 (`0.25 atm to 5 atm` + helium dilution) AND flagged the source-internal `0.25–5 atm` vs `0.25–0.5 atm` inconsistency in `limitations` + `epistemicStatus: "planned"`.
    6. **`c-0105`** (`CFI`, `ntrs-20205008449`): Source states cool-flame quasi-steady droplet combustion was first observed on ISS in CIR during **FLEX** (with n-heptane/octane/decane/dodecane), later studied in **CFI** — misattributed solely to `CFI`. Corrected investigation attribution/scope & limitations noting FLEX discovery provenance.
    7. **`c-0109`** (`ACME-BRE`, `psi-20`): Source Objective uses future tense (`"NASA - considered atmosphere for human space missions will be examined..."`) — it is a **planned** flight-program objective, whereas actual BRE PSI-20 CSV has 0 pressure column and only O2/burner-diameter/flame-count summaries. Set `epistemicStatus: "planned"` and updated claim text from confirmed past tense `"examined"` to planned objective phrasing.
    8. **`c-0113`** (`ACME-BRE`, `psi-20`): Source Objective says `"Observe burning behavior in long-duration microgravity for CH4/N2 and C2H4/N2 mixtures on porous circular burners of 25 and 50 mm diameter"` (planned ISS objective; initial validation in `ntrs-20160010073` was 5.2-s drop-tower tests). Set `epistemicStatus: "planned"`, updated past-tense `"flight tests used"` phrasing to planned objective, and noted `ntrs-20160010073` 5.2-s drop-test distinction in `limitations`.
- **EMBER v2 Resolution**: Every investigation carries `epistemicStatus: "observed" | "planned_only"` (`"planned_only"` for `FLEX-2`), and all 145 claims carry explicit `epistemicStatus` (`observed | planned | hypothesis | source_interpretation | derived_interpretation`), `verificationStatus` (`verified | corrected`), `conditionsScope`, `limitations`, `evidenceIds`, and verbatim-verified `sourceRef`.

### Fix 8 — Content Entailment Verification (Beyond `[id]` Regex Matching)
- **Legacy Defect (`web/src/lib/citations.ts:6-17`)**: `parseCitations()` checks only whether `[c-0001]` tokens exist in an allowed ID array. An LLM or summary can cite a valid ID while swapping numbers, flipping units, changing `"planned"` to `"observed"`, or dropping material/gravity constraints.
- **EMBER v2 Resolution**: Corpus chunks preserve `sectionHeader`, `tableUnits`, `epistemicStatus`, and `sourceRef` alongside 64D LSA vectors (`chunks_index.json`), enabling deterministic JEV 3-question scoring and 5-axis `VerificationReport` checking citation validity, numeric/unit matching, epistemic modality fidelity, constraint preservation, and lexical entailment.

### Fix 9 — Deterministic `groupId` Replicate & Series Grouping
- **Legacy Defect (`web/src/lib/evidence.ts:915-918`, `web/scripts/backtest.ts`)**: Replicates are guessed dynamically using continuous `withinTolerance()`, which fails to group `SAME`/`SAME-R` aged pairs (`SAME-29` & `SAME-29a` have different aging times or testIds), reused `BASS` solid samples burned sequentially across multiple test IDs (e.g., same `Sample #` `"21"`, `"25"` on progressively charred PMMA spheres), or identical day/condition replicates. Leaving paired tests ungrouped leaks near-duplicates across cross-validation folds.
- **EMBER v2 Resolution**: Every `Test` carries a deterministic `groupId` linking:
  - Same-sample reused sequences (`BASS` / `BASS-II` matching `inv + fuel + geometry + Sample #` when finite/explicit),
  - Aged smoke pairs (`SAME` / `SAME-R` base test + `'a'` secondary-aged pair or shared `sampleId`),
  - Exact/rounded condition-signature replicates `(inv, fuel, geometry, rounded thickness/diameter, O2, pressure, initialFlow, flowDirection, diluent, diluentPct, gravityG, o2.role)`.
