# Interpret: source review, scope and evaluation boundaries

Date: 14 November 2026. This is AI-assisted team source review and a development regression. It is **not independent scientific validation**, a NASA approval, a safe-design certificate, or a held-out benchmark.

## Source basis

The evaluation labels in `eval/interpret/cases.json` were frozen before the first execution of the new `compareTests` function. Initial fixture SHA-256: `dba126774f5bd7b8f3393b14c43be6af6dc143518cfb31c80661973cd2a7aa79`. All records retain the corpus source locator and source reference. Expected verdicts were chosen from source endpoint meaning and recorded factor differences; missing values remain missing. Labels were not obtained from a model's confidence or the implementation's verdict. Source-family grouping is intentionally conservative; no development/test split or independent-experiment count is claimed.

Primary materials examined:

- NASA/TM-20210011385, §2.1.1 and §2.1.2.1, report pp. 13–15; local `data/raw/ntrs/TM-20210011385.txt`. [NASA record](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20210011385). PMMA films are 1–2 cm wide and 0.1–0.4 mm thick. Establishing flame, abruptly cutting flow, and gradually approaching an extinction boundary are different operations. Low-velocity calibration, wall effects, and time lag are explicitly uncertain; the report's source interpretation must retain those caveats. Appendix A supplies as-run observation comments and geometry/oxygen values. A missing camera or outcome comment cannot establish a failed ignition.
- PSI-25 BASS-II experimental CSV and report Appendix A reconstruction. [NASA PSI](https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/data/investigations/PSI-25). B1 reports ignition/spread then flow shutoff; B2's comment reports camera problems, not ignition outcome. Width and thickness must remain distinct.
- PSI-26 BASS experimental CSV. [NASA PSI](https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/data/investigations/PSI-26). `BASS-24-12-R` says did not ignite but smoked; `BASS-14-12` says ignited and burned briefly then died when igniter was removed. Those are not the same result. N2 intervention, air cutoff and wake survival cannot be collapsed to successful suppression.
- PSI-39 CFI master CSV and `CFI_DataSummary_Spreadsheet_Jan2021.xlsx`, Master Sheet and DATA. [NASA PSI](https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/data/investigations/PSI-39). Fiber No/Yes distinguishes free and tethered droplets. Hot-flame extinction diameter, hot burning constant and cool burning constant are different quantities. CFI-1206 and CFI-1207 have cool burning after hot extinction; a hot extinction alone does not establish all-combustion cessation. Null analyzed cells are not zero or a negative observation.
- PSI-69 FLEX experimental CSV (Windows-encoded source) and reconstructed analyzed targets. [NASA PSI](https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/data/investigations/PSI-69). Droplet initial size, composition, pressure and terminal disruption constrain interpretation. Presence of hot-stage results alone is not evidence of cool continuation. The exact final-stage causal mechanism is outside this review's acceptance.
- PSI-159 CFI-G normal/inverse CSVs. [NASA PSI](https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/data/investigations/PSI-159). Column `Self-ext or flow term` distinguishes SE and FT. Normal and inverse burners reverse fuel/oxidizer roles; oxygen-stream fraction is not cabin oxygen. A real source conflict occurs at normal-table row 16, `21102A1`: code SE versus comment `Manually stopped the flow. No cool flame detected.` This must be exposed as conflict, not silently resolved to natural extinction.
- PSI-101 SAME/SAME-R analyzed workbook and SAME-R experimental CSV. [NASA PSI](https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/data/investigations/PSI-101). These are non-flaming heated-sample aerosol observations. Workbook columns contain multiple diameter definitions: M1/M0 CMD, diameter of average mass, Hatch-Choate Dg and instrument-specific moments. One scalar `particleSizeNm` does not prove compatible metric definitions. Aging/sampling suffix measurements are dependent. No flame-establishment conclusion follows from smoke generation.
- PSI-107 `SPICE_FinalReport.txt`, Summary and Procedure, plus experimental CSV. [NASA PSI](https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/data/investigations/PSI-107). Smoke point is onset of soot release and its luminous flame length. Procedure adjusts fuel flow to reach that boundary; not every recorded flame length is a smoke-point measurement. Burner diameter, coflow and composition matter; flame length does not establish particle size or detector response.

## Endpoint and essential-factor matrix

| Family | Interpretable endpoint in this release | Essential factors and boundaries |
|---|---|---|
| BASS/BASS-II | Flame establishment, sustained spread, spread rate, operator flow termination versus quench/blowoff | Material/phase, film/slab/rod/sphere geometry, thickness/width/diameter, support, O2 role/fraction, pressure, flow direction/history and event segment, ignition/sample history, platform/gravity. No universal numerical tolerance is scientifically justified. |
| CFI/FLEX | Droplet burning-rate constant, hot extinction and separately evidenced cool continuation | Fuel mixture, initial diameter, tether/support, gas composition/diluent, pressure, gravity/mode, temporal hot/cool stage, disruption. No transfer from hot extinction to complete cessation. |
| ACME-CFI-G | Recorded hot ignition, hot extinction/cool continuation only when endpoint evidence is present | Normal/inverse mode, fuel and oxidizer roles/composition, mass-flow setting, burner geometry/thermal history, O2 history, pressure, event/intervention. Contradictory code/comment blocks a natural-extinction interpretation. |
| SAME/SAME-R | Recorded smoke particle-size observations | Material/pyrolysis heater, inlet flow, aging/mixing duration, sampling/instrument/diameter definition, dilution and gravity. No ignition/extinction claim. Numeric subtraction requires matching definitions. |
| SPICE | Recorded smoke-point length where target actually exists | Fuel mixture, nozzle diameter, coflow direction/speed, smoke-point versus ordinary flame-length method, gravity, pressure. No detector performance inference. |
| Saffire-II | Recorded spread-rate observations | Material, sample size/confinement, atmosphere, forced flow, geometry, flight scale; no replacement of small-scale controls or universal threshold. |
| BRE/E-FIELD | Daily aggregate context | Current rows expand daily totals into parameter rows. No per-parameter individual outcome, independence, voltage effect or causal comparison is accepted. |
| Saffire-I/III, SLICE | Catalog/context only for missing typed endpoints | Accessible metadata remains visible; absent endpoint events are not invented from objectives/terminal text. |
| FLEX-2 | Planned context only | No observed rows in this corpus. Planned studies cannot provide observed comparisons. |

Machine-readable inventory of all **15** investigations is `eval/interpret/coverage-manifest.json`. Available event types are inventory, not proof that every event is scientifically accepted. The manifest lists limitations per investigation, actual evaluation case IDs, and explicit context-only families. Passing cases from one family does not validate the others.

## How to reproduce and read results

Run `npx tsx eval/interpret/run.ts` from the project root. It calls the pure comparison function without a server, AI credentials, retrieval or network. `eval/interpret/report.json` contains frozen-label accuracy, confusion matrix, class F1, useful-comparison recall, critical errors and every full comparison/reason. A/B reversal checks invariance and numerical sign; it does not add independent cases. Unsupported classes have null F1 rather than silently perfect scores. `usefulComparisonRecall` measures whether a source-supported descriptive result was returned; it is not correctness of AI prose.

The 25 cases include 23 primary-table development regressions, one aggregate audit and one source-conflict audit. Cases are source backed but not 25 independent experiments. Synthetic software mutations, when present in engine unit tests, are robustness tests and are **not added** to source-case scientific accuracy. The report deliberately does not claim external review, closed-test independence, same-generator baseline superiority, live-LLM explanation accuracy or complete scientific acceptance. These gates require work beyond this regression and must remain visible.

## Acceptance decision

No favorable score here can override a critical source conflict, unsupported causal transfer, invented observation or metric-definition mismatch. The engine may offer useful conditional/descriptive observations while refusing a causal/numeric claim. A low score remains visible and is not repaired by relabelling to match outputs. Changes to fixture expectations require an explicit source correction with rationale and a new fixture version/hash.

## Explicit post-run source correction

The initial run was 23/25 (accuracy 0.92, macro-F1 over present classes 0.8041), useful comparisons 12/12, and one critical smoke-versus-flame error. Original files remain `cases-initial-v1.json` and `report-initial-v1.json`. Saffire-II 2-5/2-6 was initially annotated as different dimensions; re-reading PSI-99 CSV rows 6/7 shows identical dimensions and ignition settings, plus an inferred material for 2-6. Version 1.1 corrects that label to conditional and explicitly records this post-run correction. It is not an untouched heldout benchmark. No comparable-class source example is claimed by this small set; its macro-F1 excludes zero-support classes and does not validate that class.

Version 1.2 corrects a second source annotation: SAME-R CSV rows 2/3 explicitly record net-aging times 38 s and 261 s. The initial conditional label omitted this known essential difference; a descriptive-only label is appropriate while atmospheric conditions remain assumed. The original and v1.1 fixtures remain available. These two annotation errors are material limits of single AI-assisted team review and further prevent an independent-scientific-validation claim.

## Expanded source-backed numerical checks (development)

Two additional arithmetic cases were frozen **before** the numerical engine change in `eval/interpret/numeric-cases.json` (SHA-256 `8763d0d21b42269b1adac16caad00d42ca188183d9f776fd4448d9315da35ec2`). ## Same-generator live diagnostic (explicit opt-in)

`npx tsx eval/interpret/compare-generators.ts` makes **no requests**. `--live` explicitly enables paid OpenAI calls using the existing server-side credential resolver; no key is printed or written in reports. Optional `--case-ids=id1,id2` selects frozen cases; `--out=path` chooses the report. Defaults cover eight mixed positive, missing-endpoint, hot/cool, smoke and source-conflict cases. Both tracks use gpt-6-luna, the same prompt, same exact records/source-method references and query; only the structured-comparison track adds the engine ComparisonResult. Expected labels/rationales are used only after model generation and never enter either prompt. Two concurrent jobs, 800 completion tokens, 30-second deadlines, no retries or model substitutions bound the run. Errors and truncated/invalid outputs remain visible per response.

This is a **development diagnostic of whether structured decisions improve consistency of the same generator**. It is not a retrieval benchmark, an external-competitor comparison, independent scientific validation or expert evaluation of free-text correctness. Tiny-sample differences must be reported as counts; agreement in the structured arm does not independently validate the engine supplying those labels. Domain experts must still review semantic source support and explanations. No live calls were made by the evaluation subagent.
