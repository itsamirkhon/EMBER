## Coverage and science caveats
- Compact JSON currently contains **1,678** PSI records; **1,586** have both O₂ and pressure values for the map. Missing rows must never be presented as plotted.
- The evidence cliff is the restricted **solid-material actual flight test with reported oxygen** subset; ground fire tests and gas-oxidizer-stream experiments are NOT evidence that elevated-O₂ cabin materials are qualified.
- `lunar-rover` at **30% O₂ / 70.3 kPa / 0.166 g** is a challenge/design scenario, **not** a NASA-certified rover cabin specification. Suit airflow is unreported. See `compact.json` mission sources and assumption fields.
- Output labels describe the results of matching the scenario against **previously recorded** tests. They do not claim an experiment was performed, material is safe, or burn probability was computed.
- `Nearest` is a normalized heuristic ranking, **not** proof that every plotted test is applicable. Opposing test outcomes are now shown separately. Source verification remains essential.
- Existing automatic scientific verification/backtests were written and run by the team; they are not independent specialist validation. Legacy seeded study data can include reconstructed test protocols and series aggregates.
